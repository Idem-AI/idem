Every figure says where it came from

Observed data, a derived estimate and an assumption are not worth the same. Blurring them would give a false impression of precision.

Data

An observed, published figure with its source and its date. Fuel prices, urban density, exchange rates.

Estimate

A value derived from comparable data, when nothing direct exists for your market.

Assumption

A deliberate choice by the engine, flagged as such, because no reliable data was available.

What could make this project succeed or fail, and what should I change?

What IDEM Simulator does not promise you

The results are estimates based on the data, assumptions and scenarios available at the time of analysis.

A high index guarantees neither viability, nor profitability, nor the long-term survival of your business. A low index does not mean the project is impossible: it means that, in the scenarios that were run, some factors carry significant risk.

It is a decision-support tool. Not a prediction, not a guarantee.
