# Landing pages

Design is the product here. A landing page that reads as a template has already lost, regardless of what the copy says.

## Section architecture

Choose sections because this product needs them, not because landing pages have them. A focused page with four sections beats the full nine-section catalogue every time.

Available moves: hero, proof, the problem stated concretely, how it works, capability detail, pricing, objection handling, closing action. Pick what the product actually needs.

**Vary the shape.** If section one is a centred stack, section two is not. Alternate: full-bleed against contained, text-led against image-led, one column against two uneven ones. The design system's page cadence tells you the rhythm to follow.

## Hero

The headline says what the product does for whom, in the reader's words. Not a slogan, not a category label.

- Good: "Send invoices your clients can pay with MoMo in under a minute."
- Banned: "Empowering businesses to reach their full potential."

One primary action. A secondary link is allowed, styled clearly below the primary. Never two buttons of equal weight.

If a product screenshot exists, it belongs above the fold and it must be real content, never a grey wireframe box.

## Proof

Specific beats abundant. One named customer with a concrete number outperforms six logos and three anonymous five-star quotes. Testimonials carry a real name, a role, a company and a photograph. Nothing invented that reads as verifiable fact, no fake press logos.

## Pricing

Two or three tiers maximum. Say what happens at the limits. Currency and period always visible. If one tier is recommended, mark it once and make it structurally different, not merely outlined in the accent colour.

## Footer

Real links. Legal pages, contact, social. No `href="#"` on anything a user might click.

## SEO and performance

`<title>` and `<meta name="description">` written for this product. Open Graph tags. One `<h1>` per page, headings in order. Images carry `width`, `height` and `loading="lazy"` below the fold.
