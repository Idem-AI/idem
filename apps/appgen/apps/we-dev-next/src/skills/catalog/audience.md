# Audience: Sub-Saharan Africa

This platform primarily serves Sub-Saharan Africa. Every piece of generated content reflects that. This is not decoration on top of a generic site; it is who the site is for.

## People in images

Always Black African people. Never generic Western, European or Asian stock photography.

Unsplash search terms that return usable results: `african business`, `african woman`, `african man`, `african team`, `black professional`, `african entrepreneur`, `african technology`, `african market`.

- Avatars and testimonials: a range of Black African faces, men and women, different ages.
- Team and hero photography: African teams in modern working environments.
- Use real `<img src="https://images.unsplash.com/…" alt="…">` with descriptive alt text.

## Names, places, money

- **People**: Amara Diallo, Kwame Asante, Fatou Ndiaye, Chidi Okonkwo, Aisha Mbeki, Thabo Nkosi, Adaeze Eze, Ousmane Traoré.
- **Cities**: Lagos, Nairobi, Dakar, Accra, Douala, Abidjan, Kigali, Johannesburg, Kampala, Yaoundé.
- **Currencies**: XAF/FCFA, XOF, NGN, KES, GHS, or USD where it genuinely fits. Format prices the way they are written locally.
- **Phone numbers**: +237, +234, +254, +233, +225, +221, +250.
- **Companies**: African-sounding or Africa-based names in placeholder data and client logos.

## Content and framing

- Social proof references African companies, organisations and communities.
- Success stories feature African entrepreneurs and businesses.
- Where the product touches payments, the familiar rails are mobile money (MTN MoMo, Orange Money, M-Pesa, Wave) at least as much as cards.
- Assume variable connectivity: keep images sized sensibly, avoid layouts that break before everything has loaded.

Write concretely, not about Africa in the abstract. "Invoicing for workshops in Douala and Yaoundé" is content; "empowering African businesses" is the banned marketing register.
