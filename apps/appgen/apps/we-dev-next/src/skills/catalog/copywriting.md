# Copy

Copy is the fastest tell. Generated layouts can pass; generated sentences rarely do. They share a shape: abstract subject, aspirational verb, no referent.

## The replacement rule

Every sentence names something concrete. If a sentence would still make sense pasted onto a different product's site, it is filler. Delete or rewrite it.

| Generated | Written |
|---|---|
| Streamline your workflow | Turn a quote into a paid invoice in three clicks |
| Empowering African businesses | Built for workshops and traders in Douala, Lagos and Accra |
| Seamless integration | Connects to MTN MoMo and Orange Money |
| Take your business to the next level | Know which clients still owe you, today |

## Banned vocabulary

*elevate, unlock, empower, supercharge, seamless, streamline, leverage, unleash, revolutionise, transform your, game-changer, cutting-edge, next-generation, world-class, mission-critical, robust, holistic, in today's fast-paced world, we believe that.*

Also banned: em dashes (use commas, colons, semicolons, periods, parentheses), exclamation marks outside genuine celebration, and the aphoristic cadence where every section ends on the same contrarian one-liner rhythm.

## Structure

- **Headings do not restate the section.** If the heading says "Features" and the paragraph beneath opens "Our features include", cut the paragraph's opening.
- **Front-load.** The first four words carry the meaning. Readers scan.
- **Buttons: verb plus object.** "Create invoice", "Download report", "Delete project". Never "Submit", "OK", "Click here". Two "Learn more" links on one page means neither says anything.
- **Links read standalone.** "View pricing plans", not "click here" — screen readers announce links out of context.

## Interface copy

- **Errors** say what happened and what to do: "That email is already registered. Sign in instead?" Never "An error occurred", never a raw code.
- **Empty states** say what would be here and offer the action: "No invoices yet" plus "Create your first invoice".
- **Confirmations** name the consequence: "Delete this project and its 14 files? This cannot be undone."
- **Placeholders are examples, not labels.** `+237 6 71 23 45 67`, not "Enter your phone number". The label above the field does the labelling.

## Language

Write in the language requested for the interface. Placeholder data, testimonials and demo content follow that same language, including names and address formats.
